# ⚖️ Legal AI Suite by Hitesh Gottapu

An integrated suite of AI-powered Streamlit apps built to assist legal professionals, clients, and researchers with document analysis, legal drafting, and legal clarifications. Powered by Google Gemini LLM, Hugging Face Transformers, and modern UI/UX practices.

## 🧩 Features Overview

### ⚖️ Legal Clarifier
- Canadian legal Q&A using Gemini AI
- Jurisdiction & law type dropdowns
- Real-time streamed answers
- References to relevant statutes/case law
- Modern UI with cards & tooltips  
✅ [GitHub](https://github.com/Hiteshgottapu/Legal-Clarifier)

### 📄 Document Analyzer
- Upload and read PDF/DOCX
- Semantic Q&A over document content
- Summarization (facebook/bart-large-cnn)
- TF-IDF keyword extraction
- Token count + clean UI  
✅ [GitHub](https://github.com/Hiteshgottapu/Document-Analyzer)

### 📝 Legal Drafter Pro
- Guided legal document generation (NDA, Will, etc.)
- Smart prompt-based autofill
- Export to PDF, DOCX, TXT
- Gemini-based AI content generation  
✅ [GitHub](https://github.com/Hiteshgottapu/Legal_Draft)
